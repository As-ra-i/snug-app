Snug Support

Snug shrinks a video under the size you choose, right on your iPhone or iPad.

Questions, bug reports, and feedback: polymer-beaus2m@icloud.com

If something went wrong, include your device model, iOS version, the original video size and length, and the target size. You don’t need to send the video.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the result smaller than my target?
Snug leaves a little headroom so the result never goes over the target. Snug counts 1 MB as 1,000,000 bytes, which is always at or below what apps allow.
Does Snug change or delete my original video?
No. The result is always saved as a new item in Photos or a new file in the folder you chose.
What happens if I leave the app while compressing?
On iOS 26 or later, compression keeps going and shows its progress in the system. On earlier versions, keep Snug open until compression finishes. If you force quit Snug, the compression stops and you need to start it again.
The result wasn’t saved to Photos.
Open Settings, go to Privacy & Security, Photos, Snug, and allow adding photos. Then tap Save Again on the result screen.
Why can’t I convert HDR to SDR?
Keep the HEVC codec with Keep HDR turned on. HDR videos are then compressed as HDR.
